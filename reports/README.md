Option A (revoke `authenticated` writes) is **not recommended**: it needs 56 INVOKER functions converted to DEFINER, replacing RLS with 56 hand-written tenant checks — the second authorization system canon 35 forbids. **Refinement 1:** clause 3 needs a *decision procedure*, because “has no business invariant” is not decidable by inspection — FIN-8, FIN-10 and QUO-1 all looked fine. Applying it immediately found two more: **FIN-10** — `record_payment` refuses to over-allocate and takes an advisory lock doing it, yet a `finance_manager` put **1300 against a 1000 invoice** by direct DML, still reporting `partially_paid`, with `customer_outstanding` deriving the customer's balance from it; guarded on **both** sides, since the total can also shrink beneath the allocations. **QUO-1** — `quotations.total_amount` is *defined* as the sum of its items and maintained on one path, so an ordinary employee left a quotation reading **1000 while its items summed to 6000**: the price offered to a customer, and the number `advance_quotation` carries into the booking. **Recomputed rather than refused**, because a derived value with no independent source can simply be kept right. **GOV-7** makes CUST-1's consumer-impact rule durable without a new governance document: `AGENTS.md §3 5b` gains a second question — *which code consumes, parses or derives from the structure you changed?* — guarded narrowly by pinning the set of catalog-driven functions, of which ORVION has exactly one. **FIN-11** recorded rather than patched, because FIN-9 asks the identical question and the two want one answer.
