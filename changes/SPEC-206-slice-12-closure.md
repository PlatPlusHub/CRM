## Business Reason

`SPEC-203` reproduced and repaired two measured defects on `public.users` — **USR-1**, the only member of the canon-34 identity-and-access family with no emitter at all, and **USR-2**, a step-up charged by the RPC and by nothing at the table door. So the engineering is live, the blocker is repaired, and what is still missing is certification. This contract exists to provide it at its true size, and to avoid for the third time the failure mode that has now cancelled three contracts in a row: freezing an obligation without first proving it reachable.
